Family Activities at Grand Slam Entertainment

Grand Slam Entertainment is built to work across a wide age range in one family visit, which is the main challenge with "things to do with kids in Branson" — a lot of attractions skew toward either toddlers or teens, and it's hard to find one spot where a 6-year-old and a 16-year-old both have a good time. Grand Slam has three separate attractions under one roof at Ballparks of America, so different family members can gravitate toward what actually fits them instead of everyone doing the same thing.

What Tends to Fit Which Age

  • Younger kids — Grand Slam Glow Mini Golf is designed for all ages and is especially popular with young children. The blacklight course gives it a different feel than a typical outdoor mini golf stop.
  • Older kids and teens — Operation Outbreak Zombie Laser Tag and The Skeleton Key Escape Rooms tend to land better with this age group.
  • The whole family together — the Triple Play bundle covers all three attractions, so a family can split up by preference and regroup, or go through everything together.

We don't have a documented minimum age requirement to share here, so if you have a very young child and want to know whether a specific attraction is a good fit, call Grand Slam at (417) 343-6540 before you visit.

Practical Notes for Families

  • Wear comfortable clothes and closed-toe shoes. White and neon-colored clothing glows under the blacklights on the golf course, which kids tend to enjoy.
  • Plan on 2–3 hours if you're doing all three attractions, less if you're just doing one or two.
  • Everything is indoors and climate-controlled, so the visit isn't dependent on the weather that day.
  • Free on-site parking is available at Ballparks of America.

Located at Ballparks of America in Branson

Grand Slam Entertainment is at 1000 Pat Nash Dr, Branson, MO 65616. The facility is ADA accessible; contact Grand Slam in advance if your family has specific accessibility needs.

Pricing & Booking

Individual attractions are $10–$25 per person, with the Double Play ($18/person) and Triple Play ($35/person) bundles available for families who want more than one attraction. Reserving online holds your date and time; you pay when you arrive.
