Grand Slam Entertainment FAQ

Location, hours, pricing, reservations, groups, and accessibility — everything guests ask us most.

Reserve Your Spot Explore Attractions

Answers below cover location and parking, attraction options, hours, reservations, groups, pricing, and accessibility. If your question isn't answered here, call us at (417) 343-6540.

Our Triple Play package is $35 per person and includes all three attractions: Glow Golf, Operation Outbreak Zombie Laser Tag, and The Skeleton Key Escape Rooms. Individual attractions and the Double Play combo are also available — see our pricing page for the full breakdown.
We are located at Ballparks of America, 1000 Pat Nash Dr, Branson, MO 65616 — easily accessible in Branson's entertainment district with free on-site parking.
Absolutely. Grand Slam Entertainment is designed for all ages. The glow golf course is especially popular with young children, while laser tag and escape rooms are great for older kids, teens, and adults.
Walk-ins are welcome, but we strongly recommend booking in advance — especially on weekends and peak Branson season. Reserving online guarantees your spot. Booking online reserves your date and time; there's no payment required to reserve — you pay when you arrive.
Yes! The Triple Play package gets you into all three attractions and you can experience them in any order. Most guests complete all three in 2–3 hours.
Yes! We host birthday parties, corporate team events, school groups, and private outings. Contact us to discuss group pricing and private booking availability.
Hours change seasonally. July and August (through Aug 16): Monday–Sunday, 12pm–10pm, closed Thursdays. August 17–September 10: closed. Starting September 11: Fridays and Saturdays, 6pm–Midnight. October: Thursday–Sunday, 6pm–11pm. Pick a date on our booking page for exact available times.
Glow Golf typically takes 45–60 minutes for 18 holes. Laser Tag sessions run about 20–30 minutes. Escape Rooms are 60-minute experiences. Plan on 2–3 hours total for all three.
Yes! Our facility is ADA accessible. The glow golf course and laser tag arena are designed to be inclusive. Please contact us in advance if your group has specific accessibility needs and we'll make sure everything is ready for you.
Wear comfortable clothes — especially for laser tag. White and neon-colored clothing looks amazing under the UV blacklights in the glow golf course! Closed-toe shoes are recommended for all attractions.
Absolutely! You can purchase Glow Golf ($10/person), Laser Tag ($10/person), or Escape Rooms ($25/person) individually, or grab the Double Play combo — Glow Golf + Laser Tag for $18/person — or the Triple Play bundle for all three at $35/person.
Contact Grand Slam directly at (417) 343-6540 to discuss pricing and availability for larger groups, teams, and youth organizations.
