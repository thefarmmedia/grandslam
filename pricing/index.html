Groups & Parties

For birthday parties, corporate events, school groups, or other larger groups, contact Grand Slam directly at (417) 343-6540 to discuss pricing and availability. We haven't published a separate group rate here since we want this page to only show pricing we can confirm is current.

How Booking Works

Booking online reserves your date and time — there's no payment required to reserve, and you pay when you arrive. Walk-ins are welcome, but reserving in advance is recommended, especially on weekends and during peak Branson season.
