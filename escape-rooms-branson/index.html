A 60-Minute Themed Escape Room

The Skeleton Key is Grand Slam Entertainment's escape room experience: a 60-minute, immersive, themed room built into the same indoor complex as the glow golf course and laser tag arena. It's designed as a self-contained experience — you don't need to book Glow Golf or Laser Tag to do the escape room, though many guests combine all three in one visit.

We're not going to invent details about specific puzzles, difficulty level, or room capacity here beyond what's confirmed above — if you want specifics before you book, call Grand Slam directly at (417) 343-6540.

Who It's For

  • Couples looking for something more interactive than dinner and a show.
  • Groups — corporate teams, school and youth groups, and families work through the room together.
  • Anyone visiting Ballparks of America looking for a way to spend an hour indoors between games.

Located at Ballparks of America in Branson

Grand Slam Entertainment is at 1000 Pat Nash Dr, Branson, MO 65616, inside Ballparks of America, with free on-site parking. The facility is ADA accessible; contact Grand Slam in advance if your group has specific accessibility needs.

Pricing & Booking

The Skeleton Key Escape Rooms cost $25 per person on their own, or you can get all three attractions — Glow Golf, Zombie Laser Tag, and Escape Rooms — in the Triple Play bundle for $35 per person, the best value if you want the full visit.

Booking online reserves your date and time; there's no payment required to reserve — you pay when you arrive.
