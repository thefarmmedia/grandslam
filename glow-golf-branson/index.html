Glow Golf in Branson, MO

An 18-hole blacklight mini golf course at Ballparks of America — neon murals, UV-lit obstacles, and a round that plays the same rain or shine.

Reserve Your Spot Explore Attractions

An 18-Hole Blacklight Course

Grand Slam Glow Mini Golf is a full 18-hole course built entirely indoors and lit with blacklight rather than daylight. The obstacles, murals, and greens are painted to glow under UV light, so the course looks and plays differently than a typical outdoor Branson mini golf stop — it's less about mountain-view scenery and more about neon color and atmosphere as you play.

Because the whole course is indoors and climate-controlled, the round doesn't change with the weather or the time of day. A 6pm tee time in October looks the same as a noon round in July.

Who It's For

The course is built to work for a wide range of visitors:

  • Families with young kids — glow golf is one of the most popular attractions with younger children.
  • Couples looking for something to do together besides another dinner reservation.
  • Groups and larger parties, whether that's a birthday party, a school group, or a family reunion.
  • Baseball teams and families at Ballparks of America — since Grand Slam is located inside the same complex, it's a natural way to fill downtime between games or tournaments without leaving the property.
  • Anyone avoiding the weather — the course doesn't close for rain, heat, or cold since it's entirely indoors.

Located at Ballparks of America in Branson

Grand Slam Entertainment is located at 1000 Pat Nash Dr, Branson, MO 65616, inside Ballparks of America, with free on-site parking. Walk-ins are welcome, but reserving a date and time in advance is recommended, especially on weekends and during peak Branson season.

Pricing & Booking

Glow Golf is $10 per person on its own. It's also included in the Double Play combo (Glow Golf + Zombie Laser Tag) for $18 per person, and in the Triple Play bundle (all three attractions) for $35 per person — the best value if you want to see everything in one visit.

Booking online reserves your date and time; there's no payment required to reserve — you pay when you arrive.
