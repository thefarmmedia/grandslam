Rainy Day Activities in Branson, MO

Three fully indoor attractions at Ballparks of America that keep running no matter what the forecast says.

A Rain Plan That Doesn't Depend on the Forecast

We're not going to tell you it rains constantly in Branson, because it doesn't — but on any multi-day trip, there's a real chance at least one afternoon doesn't cooperate. When that happens, having an indoor option already in mind saves you from scrambling. Grand Slam Entertainment's three attractions — Glow Golf, Zombie Laser Tag, and Escape Rooms — are all fully indoors at Ballparks of America, so none of them get rained out, delayed, or cut short by weather.

What "Indoor" Actually Means Here

Grand Slam Glow Mini Golf is sometimes assumed to be an outdoor course because most mini golf in Branson is. It isn't — it's an 18-hole course built entirely indoors and lit with blacklight and neon murals rather than natural light. Operation Outbreak Zombie Laser Tag and The Skeleton Key Escape Rooms are both indoor attractions as well. None of the three attractions have an outdoor component, so a storm outside doesn't change anything about how they run.

Making the Most of a Rainy Afternoon

  • If you only have time for one attraction, Glow Golf is the most requested pick for a rain-day visit since it works for the widest age range.
  • If you want to fill more of the afternoon, the Triple Play bundle covers all three attractions, and most guests finish in about 2–3 hours.
  • Rainy days in Branson tend to push more visitors toward indoor attractions at once, so reserving your date and time online ahead of time is worth doing if you can.

Located at Ballparks of America in Branson

Grand Slam Entertainment is at 1000 Pat Nash Dr, Branson, MO 65616, with free on-site parking. Walk-ins are welcome, but reservations are recommended, especially when the forecast is bad.

Pricing & Booking

Glow Golf and Laser Tag are $10 per person, Escape Rooms are $25 per person, and the Double Play ($18/person) and Triple Play ($35/person) bundles cover more than one attraction. Reserving online holds your date and time; you pay when you arrive.
