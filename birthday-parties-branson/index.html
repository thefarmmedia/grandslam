Birthday Parties at Grand Slam Entertainment

Grand Slam hosts birthday parties at its Ballparks of America location in Branson, alongside corporate team events, school groups, and private outings. Because there are three separate attractions under one roof — Glow Golf, Zombie Laser Tag, and The Skeleton Key Escape Rooms — a birthday group can pick whichever mix fits the birthday kid (or adult) best, rather than being locked into one activity.

We don't publish a separate fixed birthday party package on this page, since we don't want to advertise pricing or inclusions we can't confirm are current. For group pricing and private booking availability for a birthday party, contact Grand Slam directly at (417) 343-6540.

Building a Birthday Visit

  • Younger kids tend to gravitate toward the glow golf course — it's built for all ages and is one of the most popular attractions with young children.
  • Older kids and teens often lean toward Zombie Laser Tag or The Skeleton Key Escape Rooms.
  • Mixed-age groups can do all three with the Triple Play bundle, which most guests complete in about 2–3 hours.
  • Because everything is indoors and climate-controlled, a birthday visit isn't dependent on the weather.

Located at Ballparks of America in Branson

Grand Slam Entertainment is at 1000 Pat Nash Dr, Branson, MO 65616, inside Ballparks of America, with free on-site parking. Walk-ins are welcome, but we'd recommend reserving your date and time in advance for a birthday group, especially on weekends.

Pricing & Booking

Individual pricing is $10 per person for Glow Golf, $10 for Laser Tag, and $25 for Escape Rooms, with the Double Play ($18/person) and Triple Play ($35/person) bundles available if your group wants more than one attraction. For a larger birthday party or group pricing, contact Grand Slam directly.
