Group Activities in Branson

Grand Slam Entertainment hosts groups of all kinds at its Ballparks of America location — corporate team events, school groups, church and youth groups, family reunions, and large families, in addition to birthday parties. Because there are three distinct attractions on site, a group can split up (some playing glow golf while others do laser tag) or move through all three together.

Who Books Group Visits

  • Baseball and sports teams — since Grand Slam is inside Ballparks of America, it's a convenient way for teams and families to fill time between games or during tournament downtime without leaving the complex.
  • School and youth groups — the mix of physical (laser tag), skill-based (glow golf), and puzzle-based (escape rooms) attractions gives a group options for different interests.
  • Corporate groups looking for a team outing away from a typical meeting room.
  • Church and youth groups, family reunions, and other large families visiting Branson together.

We're not going to invent specific group discount tiers or minimum headcounts here. If you're planning a group visit, call Grand Slam directly at (417) 343-6540 to discuss pricing and availability for your group's size and date.

Located at Ballparks of America in Branson

Grand Slam Entertainment is at 1000 Pat Nash Dr, Branson, MO 65616, with free on-site parking and full ADA accessibility. Contact Grand Slam in advance if your group has specific accessibility needs.

Pricing & Booking

Standard per-person pricing applies to group visits: $10 for Glow Golf, $10 for Laser Tag, $25 for Escape Rooms, or the Double Play ($18/person) and Triple Play ($35/person) bundles. For group-specific pricing, contact Grand Slam directly rather than booking online.
