An Alternative to Dinner and a Show

Branson does dinner-and-a-show better than almost anywhere, but it's not the only way to spend an evening together. Grand Slam Entertainment gives couples something more interactive: an 18-hole blacklight golf course, a zombie-themed laser tag arena, and a 60-minute escape room, all indoors at Ballparks of America. It works well on its own or as something to do before or after dinner.

Three Different Kinds of Date

  • Glow Golf — low-key and easy to talk through, with the blacklight course giving it a different atmosphere than a typical round of mini golf.
  • The Skeleton Key Escape Rooms — a 60-minute room you work through together, which tends to be a good option for couples who want to problem-solve as a team.
  • Zombie Laser Tag — for couples who'd rather compete than cooperate, Operation Outbreak adds some friendly rivalry to the evening.

You can book one attraction for a shorter date, or the Triple Play bundle if you want to turn it into the whole evening.

Located at Ballparks of America in Branson

Grand Slam Entertainment is at 1000 Pat Nash Dr, Branson, MO 65616, with free on-site parking. Walk-ins are welcome, but reserving your date and time online is recommended for weekend evenings.

Pricing & Booking

Glow Golf and Laser Tag are $10 per person, Escape Rooms are $25 per person. For two people doing all three, the Triple Play bundle is $35 per person. Reserving online holds your date and time; you pay when you arrive.

Date Night FAQ

Any of the three attractions work for a couple — Glow Golf is a relaxed, low-pressure option, Escape Rooms give you something to solve together, and Zombie Laser Tag adds some competition.
A single attraction runs anywhere from about 20 minutes (laser tag) to an hour (escape rooms) or 45–60 minutes (glow golf). Doing all three takes most guests about 2–3 hours.
