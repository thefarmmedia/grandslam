Zombie Laser Tag in Branson, MO

Operation Outbreak: an indoor, zombie-apocalypse-themed laser tag arena at Ballparks of America with state-of-the-art gear.

Reserve Your Spot Explore Attractions

A Zombie-Themed Laser Tag Arena

Operation Outbreak is Grand Slam Entertainment's laser tag experience, built around a zombie-apocalypse theme rather than a generic arena. Players use state-of-the-art laser tag gear inside an indoor arena designed around that theme, which gives the games a different feel than a standard black-lit laser tag room.

A typical session runs about 20–30 minutes, which makes it easy to fit into a visit alongside Glow Golf and the Skeleton Key Escape Rooms.

Who It's For

  • Older kids, teens, and adults tend to get the most out of the zombie theme and the pace of the games, though Grand Slam is built to work for all ages.
  • Groups — birthday parties, school and youth groups, and corporate outings all work well with a laser tag session as part of the visit.
  • Teams at Ballparks of America — since laser tag is inside the same complex, it's a convenient option between games without leaving the property.
  • Anyone who wants an indoor option regardless of the weather outside, since the arena is fully indoors.

Located at Ballparks of America in Branson

Grand Slam Entertainment is at 1000 Pat Nash Dr, Branson, MO 65616, inside Ballparks of America, with free on-site parking. Walk-ins are welcome, but reserving your date and time online is recommended, especially on weekends.

Pricing & Booking

Zombie Laser Tag is $10 per person on its own. Combine it with Glow Golf in the Double Play for $18 per person, or get all three attractions — Glow Golf, Laser Tag, and Escape Rooms — in the Triple Play for $35 per person.

Booking online reserves your date and time; there's no payment required to reserve — you pay when you arrive.
